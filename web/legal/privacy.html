Privacy

Organization-hosted records

The books and worker portals send sign-in details and authorized actions to the organization’s host. The host processes books, attachments, employee assignments, punches, correction requests, approvals, audit history and account agreement receipts in SQLite. Database files and journal sidecars may contain readable data. Host administrators with file access can read or change it. Within the portals, account roles and assignments limit access.

What leaves when you choose

For a Lite transfer, the selected encrypted backup envelope and the password you enter are sent to the organization’s server to decrypt and validate the transfer. A portable .firmquill export carries the portable books. A password-protected .fqdb archive recovers the host database, including accounts, original punches and pending work. Downloaded attachments, CSVs and prints can contain readable records.

Connection to the host

Both portals require a connection to the configured host. The app uses an HTTP-only session cookie and server-side sessions and returns authenticated responses with no-store caching. The organization controls its host, network, backup media and any infrastructure logs. Opening a feedback link hands its subject to your mail program.

Managing copies and requesting help

Contact your organization’s administrator about access, correction, retention or deletion of company and worker records. Signing out ends the session. The publisher has no built-in access to a company database. Administrators and recipients must manage host copies, recovery archives and exported files themselves.
